# Catalan formula corrections and short proofs of Groux's and Staunton's conjectures

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Applying Pieter Mostert's finite-sum identity (OEIS A119951, October 12, 2012) and the minimal-exponent theorem of Cação, Falcão and Malonek (2017, Theorem 6), we give short proofs of Groux Roland's denominator conjecture and Alexander Staunton's linked exponent conjecture in the FORMULA section of OEIS A120069 (December 11, 2010 and February 28, 2026, respectively). For every positive index, the reduced denominator of the partial sum of scaled Catalan numbers equals that of its last summand; we also give its binary-digit form. We correct an index in the companion numerator entry and a minimum expression in the exponent entry.
